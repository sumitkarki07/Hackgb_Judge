# Judge guide

1. Open HackGB JudgeHub and enter the private code given to you by an organizer. No account is needed.
2. Your dashboard lists only your assigned projects, their tables, categories and your progress. Search for a project or scan its table QR code.
3. If asked to sign in after scanning, enter your code. JudgeHub returns to that project and checks your assignment. An unassigned project cannot be evaluated.
4. Meet the team, watch the demo, and choose a score for **every** criterion. Scores start unselected. Read each category's rubric; sponsor criteria may differ from the overall rubric.
5. Add optional notes and select **Submit Evaluation**. Wait for the saved confirmation before moving on. If a connection fails, keep the form open and retry. Repeated submission will not create duplicate scores.
6. If you made a mistake, ask an organizer to reopen the evaluation. Its original version stays in the history. Submit the correction while judging is open.
7. After completing your category assignments, choose **Your top projects** and rank your favorites. Choose each project once. First earns 3 points, second 2, third 1. If fewer than three are available, the event's stated policy applies.
8. Sign out when done, especially on a shared device. Your session normally lasts 12 hours; code replacement or organizer revocation signs you out sooner.

An unassigned-project message means you should visit another assigned team or contact an organizer. If judging is closed, new evaluations and rankings are disabled. You never need direct access to the Google Sheet.
